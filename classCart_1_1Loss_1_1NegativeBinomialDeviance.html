Detailed Description

template<std::floating_point FloatType>
class Cart::Loss::NegativeBinomialDeviance< FloatType >

Negative binomial (of parameter \(\alpha\)) deviance.

The deviance is defined as:

\begin{eqnarray*} d(y, w) &=& \frac{1}{W}\sum_{i=1}^nw_i\left(\frac{1}{\alpha}\log\frac{1+\alpha\hat\pi(y, w)}{1+\alpha y} + y\log\frac{y(1+\alpha\hat\pi(y, w))}{\hat\pi(y, w)(1 + \alpha y)}\right) \\ &=& \frac{1}{W}\left(\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}_0}w_i\right)\log(1+\alpha\hat\pi(y, w)) + \frac{1}{W}\sum_{k=1}^K\left[\left(\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}_k}w_i\right)\left(\frac{1}{\alpha}\log\frac{1+\alpha\hat\pi(y, w)}{1+\alpha k} + k\log\frac{k(1+\alpha\hat\pi(y, w))}{\hat\pi(y, w)(1+\alpha k)}\right)\right]. \end{eqnarray*}
